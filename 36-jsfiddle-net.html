Quick Best Friend Quiz Widgets You Can Code Today

You do not need to build a whole app to make an interactive best friend quiz. Small, focused widgets, a single question card, a live score counter, a share button, can be coded in an afternoon and dropped into any page. Each one is a tiny project with a visible payoff.

Widgets are also the perfect way to practice. Every widget teaches one skill: handling clicks, updating state, or rendering results.

A One-Question Widget

Start with the smallest useful piece: a card that asks one question and offers four answers. When the visitor clicks an answer, the widget reveals whether they were right and shows a one-line explanation with the backstory.

This single widget already captures the whole spirit of a quiz. To test your friendship properly you only need a good question and an honest answer, and this widget delivers exactly that with almost no code.

A Live Score Counter

Next, add a score counter that follows the player across multiple questions. It needs three things: a current score display, a question counter, and a small progress bar that fills as the quiz advances.

Keep the logic simple: add one point for each correct answer, never subtract. A counter that only goes up keeps the mood playful, even for players who are getting everything wrong.

A Share-Your-Result Button

The most valuable widget is the one that spreads your quiz. A share button takes the final score, builds a short message like "I scored 8 out of 10 on the best friend quiz," and opens the share dialog for the player's favorite app.

Pre-fill the message but let players edit it before sending. People share more when the text feels like their own words rather than an advertisement.

Widget Ideas to Try Next

Once the basics work, extend your toolkit with one of these:

  • A countdown timer that adds pressure to each question
  • A hint button that removes two wrong answers per round
  • A leaderboard that stores top scores in the browser
  • A daily question widget that rotates in a new question each day

Build them one at a time, test each with a friend, and keep the ones that get laughs. A handful of small widgets beats one giant app that never ships.
